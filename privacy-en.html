Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-22

Poem Scroll ("the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is handled.

1. We collect nothing

  • No accounts, no sign-in, and no collection of name, email, phone number, location, device identifiers or contacts.
  • No analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no third-party tracking tools.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites, so the app never asks for tracking permission.

2. Everything stays on your device

The artwork, pinyin, meanings, notes, appreciation and narration text ship inside the app. Narration uses the built-in iPadOS speech synthesizer; no text is sent to a server. The app does not use the network and uploads nothing.

3. Permissions

The app requests no system permissions: no camera, microphone, photo library, location or notifications.

4. Children

The app is designed for families and children. Because it collects no personal information and contains no ads or analytics, it meets children's privacy requirements. Purchases are gated by App Store parental controls (Ask to Buy).

Purchases

PoemScroll Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase that unlocks the full set of scenes. It is not a subscription and does not renew. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never receive your name, payment method or card details, only whether the unlock is active. The app connects to the network only for StoreKit purchases, restore, and the in-app policy pages hosted on GitHub Pages.

5. Third parties

Apart from App Store distribution and the system speech synthesizer (both provided by Apple), the app shares data with no third party.

6. Changes

If a future change affects this policy, we will update this page and the date above.

7. Contact

Questions? Email magaxy@163.com, or see Support.
