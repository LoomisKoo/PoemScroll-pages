Support

Poem Scroll: step into the painting of each poem. Forty-five classic Chinese poems, each a living paper-cut scroll — tap anything and it moves.

Contact us

For problems or suggestions, email magaxy@163.com with your iPadOS version and device model. We usually reply within a few days.

How to use

  1. Browse the shelves by season, dynasty or poet, then tap a cover to open the scroll.
  2. Tap anything in the painting — trees, people, boats, clouds, the moon — each reacts on its own.
  3. The card in the lower right holds the poem lines; tap a line to hear it read, and tap "诗解" at the top right for meaning, notes and appreciation.

FAQ

No sound, or a character is read wrong?

Reading uses the built-in Chinese system voice and works offline. If it is silent, check the side mute switch and volume. If no Chinese voice is installed, download one in iPadOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Chinese.

Why iPad only?

The scrolls are 4:3 landscape paintings; on a phone most of the picture would be cropped away. Please use your iPad in landscape.

Does it need the internet or an account?

No account is needed, and reading poems works offline: all art, narration and notes live on the device. No ads and no personal data collected. Network access is only used for StoreKit purchases, restore, and the in-app policy pages.

What is free?

"Spring Dawn" (春晓) and "Ode to the Goose" (咏鹅) are free. All other scenes require PoemScroll Pro, a one-time purchase (not a subscription). Use Settings → Restore Purchases on a new device.

The animation stutters?

Scenes are drawn with Metal. On older devices, close other apps first; the scene pauses automatically when you switch away.
